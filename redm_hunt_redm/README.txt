redm_hunt_redm

Commands:
- /hunt <serverId>
- /stophunt

Notes:
- Built for RedM, not FiveM.
- No notifications.
- Debug goes to console with [hunt] and [hunt-client].
- The target player is the controller so the spawned sheriff riders stay relevant to that player.
- Hunters spawn mounted, ride briefly, then dismount and fight on foot.
- When the target dies, the hunt despawns automatically.
